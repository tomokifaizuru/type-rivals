How to Play

⌨️ Tap the screen to open the keyboard (or just type on a PC).
🎯 Type the first letter of a word to lock onto it, then finish typing it.
🔥 Clear words in a row to build your combo multiplier.
❤️ You have 3 lives — a word hitting the ground costs one.
🚀 Each level makes words faster and longer.
⌫ Backspace releases the current target.
